Trade-off 1: model price vs number of cameras

Cheaper models (Sol, Terra) only succeed with all 3 cameras. Opus, Gemini and Astra also succeed with the wrist alone.

Trade-off 2: reliability vs cost

Astra is as reliable as Gemini and Opus, but costs 4–5× more per run.

Trade-off 3: tokens vs time

Tokens drive time only loosely. Inference speed (provider, standard / flex / fast tier, effort level) matters as much.
